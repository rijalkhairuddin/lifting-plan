6. Acceptance limits

MOM (from 12 Sep 2025): mobile cranes every 12 months (6 months if used to lift personnel); lifting gear every 12 months (personnel lifting cages 6 months). Check your client's own requirement before choosing "Material lift".

7. Outriggers & ground bearing

Estimate only. Rigid-base method using vehicle weight, boom self-weight at half radius and hook load, checked along the whole pick-to-deck path. Bearing values are presumed screening values. Use the manufacturer's outrigger data or a PE assessment where in doubt.
